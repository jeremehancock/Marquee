## Context

The Marquee mark is authored by hand as SVG. The play triangle is a plain
3-point polygon in two files:

| File | Path data | Triangle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/assets/logo.svg` | `M27.8 25.2 L27.8 38.8 L39 32 Z` | 11.2 × 13.6 |
| `public/assets/favicon.svg` | `M25 24 L25 40 L41 32 Z` | 16 × 16 |

`templates/layout.html.twig` carries a hand-copied inline duplicate of
`logo.svg` for the topbar; the two are byte-identical today apart from the
wrapper element, and nothing enforces that.

Both paths already carry `stroke-linejoin="round"`, which does nothing — there
is no `stroke`. That attribute is a leftover and is the trap this change has to
avoid falling into.

The five PNGs in `public/assets/icons/` are rasterized from `logo.svg`, so they
all have to be regenerated. The exact recipe was recovered by reproducing the
committed files from the current SVG:

| File | Recipe | RMSE vs committed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `icon-192.png` / `icon-512.png` | `rsvg-convert -w N -h N logo.svg` | 0 (exact) |
| `icon-{192,512}-maskable.png` | render at `floor(N × 0.72)` (138 / 368), centre on solid `#1c1e24` | 0.003 / 0.005 |
| `apple-touch-icon.png` | render at 180, composite on `#1c1e24`, `-alpha off` | 0.0001 |

The residuals are palette/antialias noise, not geometry — the recipe is right.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Kill the three needle points on the play triangle, most visibly the tip.
- Keep the mark recognisably the same shape at 16 px, 36 px (topbar), and 512 px.
- Keep the header mark, the favicon, and the raster tiles telling the same story.
- Leave the maskable safe-zone guarantee the `pwa` spec relies on intact.

**Non-Goals:**
- Any change to palette, poster geometry, fan angles, ground radius, or layout.
- Restructuring the topbar's inline-SVG duplication into a shared partial. It is
  a real smell, but fixing it is a separate change and would balloon this one.
- Introducing a build step to generate the icons. They stay committed artefacts
  regenerated by hand with the recipe above.

## Decisions

### Quadratic fillets baked into the path, not a matched stroke

The two ways to round a polygon in SVG:

1. **Matched stroke** — add `stroke="#1c1e24" stroke-width="1.6"` alongside the
   existing `stroke-linejoin="round"`. One attribute, done.
2. **Baked path** — replace each vertex with a quadratic Bézier whose control
   point *is* the original vertex, entered and left at a tangent distance `d`
   along the two adjoining edges.

Going with (2). The stroke trick is tempting given `stroke-linejoin="round"` is
already sitting there, but it inflates the shape by half the stroke width on
every side — ~0.8 units on an 11.2-unit-wide triangle, a ~7% growth of a shape
that is *knocked out* of the poster behind it. That is a bigger visual change
than the rounding itself, and it would need a compensating inset to undo,
at which point it is no simpler than (2). Baked geometry also rasterizes
identically everywhere and cannot be perturbed by a stray CSS `stroke` rule on
the inline copy in the topbar.

A quadratic fillet rather than a true arc (`A`) because the three interior
angles are near-uniform (58.75°, 58.75°, 62.5°), so the parabolic approximation
is visually indistinguishable from a circular arc here, and the path stays short
and legible.

The vestigial `stroke-linejoin="round"` gets dropped in the same edit — with the
corners baked in it is doubly meaningless, and leaving it invites the next
person to "fix" the rounding by adding the stroke.

### Radius: 1.0 (logo) and 1.2 (favicon), chosen by eye

`d = r / tan(θ/2)` at each vertex, clamped to half the shorter adjoining edge.
Candidates at 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2 / 1.5 / 2.0 were rendered at 36 px and 256 px and
compared side by side against the current hard-edged mark:

- Below 1.0 the tip is still visibly sharp — it does not read as a decision.
- Above 1.5 the tip goes blobby and the triangle starts reading as a rounded
  lozenge rather than a play glyph.
- **1.0** on the logo takes the point off while leaving the silhouette
  unmistakable. This is the "nothing too drastic" the change is asking for.

The favicon triangle is 16 units tall against the logo's 13.6 — a factor of
1.176 — so **1.2** keeps the two marks looking like the same family rather than
giving the favicon a proportionally sharper tip. Verified: at 36 px and 16 px
the softening is subtle and costs nothing in legibility.

Resulting paths (2 dp, generated rather than hand-computed):

```
logo    M27.80 26.98 L27.80 37.02 Q27.80 38.80 29.32 37.88
        L37.59 32.85 Q39.00 32.00 37.59 31.15
        L29.32 26.12 Q27.80 25.20 27.80 26.98 Z

favicon M25.00 25.94 L25.00 38.06 Q25.00 40.00 26.74 39.13
        L38.85 33.07 Q41.00 32.00 38.85 30.93
        L26.74 24.87 Q25.00 24.00 25.00 25.94 Z
```

Both stay strictly inside their original triangle, so the knockout can only
shrink — the front poster is never encroached on.

### Icons regenerated with the recovered recipe, verified by diff

Regenerate all five PNGs with the table above. The check that matters is not
"do they look right" but "did anything *other* than the triangle move": diffing
each new PNG against its committed predecessor must show change confined to the
triangle's three corners. That catches a wrong scale factor or a dropped
background flatten, which are otherwise easy to ship unnoticed.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **The topbar's inline copy drifts from `logo.svg`** → This is the most likely
  way to half-ship the change: update the asset, forget the template, and the
  tab icon and header mark disagree. The two paths must be updated in the same
  commit, and the check is a literal `grep` for the old `M27.8 25.2` across
  `public/` and `templates/` returning nothing.
- **A regenerated maskable icon silently breaks the safe zone** → The 0.72 inner
  scale is preserved exactly, and the triangle only ever shrinks inward, so the
  safe zone cannot be encroached. Re-run the archived change's circular-crop
  simulation to confirm rather than assume.
- **The rounding vanishes at 16 px** → Confirmed by render, it does not; it goes
  subtle, which is the intent. A radius large enough to be obvious at 16 px
  would be far too round at 512 px, and the 512 px tile is the one users see on
  a home screen.
- **Committed binaries make the diff noisy** → Unavoidable with hand-generated
  icons; the RMSE-vs-predecessor check is what keeps the change reviewable.

## Migration Plan

None. Static assets with no persisted state and no cache-busting contract — a
stale favicon in a browser tab resolves on its own. Rollback is `git revert`.
